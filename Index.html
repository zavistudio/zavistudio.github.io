Common questions

How long does it take?

Most websites are ready in about 7 days once we have your photos and details.

What do I need to give you?

Your business name, services or menu with prices, timings, address, phone number and 5 to 10 real photos.

Will my site be number 1 on Google?

We set up the basics so Google can find your site, but ranking takes time and we do not promise a position. We promise a clean, fast, professional website.

What about domain and hosting?

Hosting can be free for simple sites. A domain like yourname.com costs a yearly fee. We tell you the cost before you decide.

How do I pay?

Half before we start and the rest when the site is ready. Bank transfer, Easypaisa or JazzCash, in rupees at the day's dollar rate.
